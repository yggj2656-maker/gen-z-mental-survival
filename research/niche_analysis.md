# KDP Niche Analysis — Gen Z Philosophy × Psychology

## Market Overview

The intersection of philosophy, psychology, and Gen Z digital struggles is a proven, growing KDP niche. Key proof points:

- **The Anxious Generation** (Haidt) — #1 NYT bestseller, #1 Developmental Psychology on Amazon
- **Dopamine Detox** (Meurisse) — 10,000+ ratings, ~65 pages, self-published success
- **Don't Believe Everything You Think** (Nguyen) — Self-published breakout in psychology/mindfulness
- **The Let Them Theory** (Robbins) — #1 Overall Amazon 2025, co-authored with Gen Z son

## Gen Z Pain Points (Data-Backed)

| Pain Point | Data |
|---|---|
| Loneliness | 67% of Gen Z (Cigna 2025) |
| Depression (under 30) | 28%, doubled from 13% in 2017 (Gallup 2026) |
| "Excellent" mental health | 29%, down from 48% in 2012 |
| Social media perceived as negative | 48% of teens (Pew 2025), up from 32% in 2022 |
| Tried quitting social media | 52% of Gen Z in 2025 |
| Teen loneliness | ~20% ages 13-17 (WHO 2025) |

## Competing Titles & Positioning

| Book | Strength | Gap/Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| The Anxious Generation | Academic rigor, data-heavy | Can feel like a textbook; not "for" Gen Z but "about" Gen Z |
| Dopamine Detox | Short, actionable | Very light on philosophy; surface-level |
| The Daily Stoic | Ancient wisdom, daily format | Doesn't address digital-age specific problems |
| Atomic Habits | Practical, evergreen | No emotional depth; all tactics, no meaning |
| Don't Believe Everything You Think | Mindfulness + neuroscience | Narrow focus on thoughts; doesn't cover broader existential issues |

## Our Positioning

**Gap in the market**: No book combines ALL of these:
1. Philosophy (existentialism, stoicism, meaning) — with DEPTH
2. Psychology (attention, dopamine, habits, burnout) — with SCIENCE
3. Modern digital reality (doomscrolling, AI, online identity) — with SPECIFICITY
4. Written TO Gen Z, not ABOUT Gen Z
5. Practical exercises and reflection prompts
6. Emotionally resonant, not academic

### Keyword Strategy
Use **micro-niche positioning**: combine identity label (Gen Z, college student) + specific pain point (loneliness, meaninglessness) + solution format (guide, workbook, philosophy). This targets buyers with urgent, specific needs — lower competition, higher conversion.

## Pricing & KDP Strategy

- **Price point**: $2.99–$4.99 (qualifies for 70% royalty)
- **Word count**: 20,000–40,000 words total (short, punchy — matches Gen Z reading habits)
- **Per chapter**: 3,000–5,000 words
- **Format**: eBook primary, paperback secondary
- **Categories**: 2 most relevant sub-categories (e.g., Philosophy > Existentialism, Self-Help > Personal Transformation)
