# Book Title Ideas — Gen Z Philosophy × Psychology

## Top 10 Titles

### 1. **The Scroll and the Void**
*Subtitle: Why Modern Life Feels Empty and How to Find Real Meaning in a Digital World*
- Hook: Juxtaposes the physical action (scrolling) with the existential result (void)
- Keywords: meaning, digital world, empty

### 2. **Always Online, Never Here**
*Subtitle: A Philosophy of Attention, Presence, and Survival for the Exhausted Generation*
- Hook: Captures the core paradox — connected but absent
- Keywords: attention, presence, exhausted generation

### 3. **You're Not Lazy, You're Overwhelmed**
*Subtitle: The Psychology of Burnout, Dopamine, and Why Your Brain Can't Keep Up*
- Hook: Validating, counter-narrative to "lazy Gen Z" stereotype
- Keywords: burnout, dopamine, brain

### 4. **Why Nothing Feels Real Anymore**
*Subtitle: Existential Philosophy for People Who Grew Up Online*
- Hook: Direct quote from the audience; immediate recognition
- Keywords: existential philosophy, online

### 5. **The Meaning Crisis**
*Subtitle: What Philosophy and Psychology Teach Us About Surviving the 21st Century*
- Hook: Names the problem directly; implies solutions
- Keywords: meaning, philosophy, psychology, 21st century

### 6. **How to Want Things Again**
*Subtitle: Rebuilding Desire, Focus, and Purpose in the Age of Infinite Distraction*
- Hook: Emotional, specific, aspirational
- Keywords: focus, purpose, distraction

### 7. **Digital Ghosts**
*Subtitle: Identity, Loneliness, and the Search for Self in a Connected World*
- Hook: Evocative metaphor for online-only existence
- Keywords: identity, loneliness, self

### 8. **The Half-Life of Attention**
*Subtitle: Reclaiming Your Mind from Social Media, AI, and the Attention Economy*
- Hook: Scientific metaphor (half-life) applied to human experience
- Keywords: attention, social media, AI, mind

### 9. **You Are Not a Product**
*Subtitle: A Gen Z Guide to Mental Freedom, Meaning, and Life Beyond the Algorithm*
- Hook: Political/empowering; anti-algorithm sentiment
- Keywords: mental freedom, meaning, algorithm, Gen Z

### 10. **Scroll Less, Live More**
*Subtitle: Practical Philosophy and Psychology for a Generation That Deserves Better*
- Hook: Simple, actionable, affirming
- Keywords: practical philosophy, psychology, generation

---

## Title Selection Criteria

Each title was evaluated on:
1. **Emotional resonance** — Would a Gen Z reader feel seen?
2. **Keyword optimization** — Does it contain KDP-searchable terms?
3. **Differentiation** — Does it stand out from existing titles?
4. **Memorability** — Is it easy to remember and share?
5. **Subtitle SEO** — Does the subtitle explain the book and contain keywords?

## Top 3 Recommendations

1. **Always Online, Never Here** — Strongest emotional hook + keyword combo
2. **Why Nothing Feels Real Anymore** — Most likely to stop a scroller mid-scroll
3. **You're Not Lazy, You're Overwhelmed** — Highest viral potential (counter-narrative)
