# DeepSeek Prompt Templates — All Stages

## 1. Market Research

**EN:** "List 10 common psychological issues faced by Gen Z college students in 2026, drawing on trends in social media (TikTok) and news (economy, climate)."

**ZH:** "列举10个Z世代大学生常见的心理困扰，结合社交媒体（如抖音）热点和当前经济/气候危机背景。"

*Output: Key pain point list with descriptions for topic selection.*

---

## 2. Title Generation

**EN:** "Suggest 8 catchy book titles aimed at Gen Z readers, combining existential philosophy and practical psychology (e.g., loneliness, meaning)."

**ZH:** "提供8个吸引Z世代读者的书名（可以中英结合），主题围绕存在主义和生活心理学，比如数字孤独、意义感等。"

*Output: Title candidate list.*

---

## 3. Chapter Outline

**EN:** "Create a 6-chapter outline for a non-fiction book on 'Digital Loneliness and Self-Help for Young Adults'. Each chapter should have a theme and 3 key points."

**ZH:** "为《数字时代的孤独与成长：年轻人的自助指南》设计6章目录，每章包含主题和3个要点。"

*Output: Chapter titles and key points (JSON format recommended).*

---

## 4. Section Draft

**EN:** "Write a 500-word passage on the topic 'Why scrolling social media can make us feel empty', in a conversational Gen Z tone."

**ZH:** "以对话口吻（符合Z世代语气），写500字，主题'为什么刷社交媒体让人觉得空虚'。"

*Output: Chapter body draft.*

---

## 5. Examples & Exercises

**EN:** "Provide two real-life examples that illustrate feeling FOMO (fear of missing out), and suggest one practical mindfulness exercise to counter it."

**ZH:** "给出两个体现错失恐惧（FOMO）的生活例子，并设计一个简单的正念练习帮助缓解。"

*Output: Case descriptions + specific exercise steps.*

---

## 6. Gamified Tasks

**EN:** "Design a simple daily 'level-up' quest list for a student: e.g., 'Gain 10 XP by focusing on study for 30 minutes', aligning with psychological growth goals."

**ZH:** "为一名学生设计一个游戏化的日常任务表，例如'专注学习30分钟，获得10经验'等，使自律过程具有升级感。"

*Output: Task list with XP values.*

---

## 7. Keyword Research

**EN:** "List 10 long-tail search queries young college students might use for their psychological struggles (e.g., 'cause of constant anxiety')."

**ZH:** "列出10个Z世代大学生最常搜索的情绪困扰关键词（如'长期焦虑原因'等）。"

---

## Style Guidance (Add to Any Prompt)

- "请用轻松幽默、贴近网络文化的语言"
- "保持前后一致的人物/叙事风格"
- Use the `temperature` and `top_p` parameters to control creativity vs. consistency
