# Cover Design Brief

## Book Info
- **Title**: Always Online, Never Here
- **Subtitle**: A Philosophy of Attention, Presence, and Survival for the Exhausted Generation
- **Author**: Moon
- **Format**: 6" x 9" paperback (KDP standard)
- **Page count**: ~120 pages (estimated from ~26,000 words)

## Cover Specs (KDP)
- Front cover only (KDP generates spine/back from page count)
- Size: 2560 x 1600 px minimum; recommended 2700 x 1800 px (300 DPI at 6x9")
- Format: JPEG, RGB
- No text too close to edges (0.125" bleed on all sides)

## Design Direction

### Concept: "The Glowing Void"
A dark, near-black background with a single phone screen glowing in the center, but the screen shows only static — or an abstract, fragmented reflection — not any recognizable app. The phone hovers in empty space. The glow is warm but cold at the same time: amber/orange fading to blue-white at the edges.

The idea: the device that promises connection delivers only light and noise. The "always online" is visible; the "never here" is the emptiness around it.

### Alternative Concepts

**A. The Incomplete Face**
A human silhouette made of glitch lines / fragmented pixels, fading at the edges. No facial features — just the outline dissolving into noise. Clean typography over dark background.

**B. The Infinite Scroll**
Abstract vertical bands of color (screen-bright: blue, white, amber) stretched like taffy downward, warping into a dark pool at the bottom. Like content literally dissolving into nothing. No phone shown — purely abstract.

**C. Typography-First**
Plain dark cover. Title in large, slightly distressed or glitched sans-serif. Subtitle clean and small beneath. No imagery at all. Like a zine. Very Gen Z.

### Color Palette
- Primary: Deep void black (#0a0a0a or near-black)
- Accent: Screen glow — amber (#ff8c42) or blue-white (#d0e8ff)
- Typography: Off-white (#f0efe8) — not pure white, feels more human

### Typography
- Title: Bold sans-serif, possibly slightly condensed or glitched. Think: Bebas Neue, Montserrat Bold, or a custom distressed font
- Subtitle: Light weight, clean sans-serif (Inter, Source Sans Pro)
- Author: Same as subtitle, smaller

### Mood References
- NOT: bright, motivational-quote, sunrise-over-mountain
- IS: quiet, slightly unsettling, honest, late-night vibes
- Think: album art for a lo-fi artist, not a TED Talk thumbnail

### What to Avoid
- No stock photos of happy people
- No phone with Instagram/TikTok logo visible (trademark + cliché)
- No glowing brain holograms
- No motivational-quote energy
- No neon pink/cyan vaporwave (too dated, too aesthetic-bait)

## Recommended Approach
1. Generate 3-4 variations of **Concept A** (strongest metaphor — phone as false promise)
2. If unsatisfying, try **Concept B** as backup
3. Add typography in post (Photoshop/Canva) rather than relying on AI to render text correctly
4. Keep the final cover file under 50MB for KDP upload

## Typography Overlay (for post-processing)
Place title in the upper half, centered. Phone image in lower center. Subtitle beneath title, smaller. Author name "Moon" at bottom. Total text area must stay within KDP safe zone (0.25" from trim on all sides).
