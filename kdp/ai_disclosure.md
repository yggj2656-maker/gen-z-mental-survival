# AI Content Disclosure — KDP Requirements

## Amazon KDP Policy (2026)

Amazon requires disclosure of AI-generated or AI-assisted content.

- **AI-generated**: Created entirely by AI, minimal human editing
- **AI-assisted**: AI-drafted, substantially edited and reviewed by human author

## Disclosure Statement Template

For our AI-assisted workflow:

> **Author's Note on AI Assistance**
>
> Portions of this book were drafted with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools. All AI-generated content has been thoroughly reviewed, edited, and refined by the author to ensure accuracy, originality, and alignment with the book's voice and purpose. The author takes full responsibility for the final content.

## AI Content Quality Red Flags

Remove these patterns from AI-generated drafts:

- **Repetitive filler**: Same idea restated in different words → cut it
- **Hallucinated facts**: All statistics, dates, and citations must be verified
- **Generic openings**: "In today's fast-paced world..." → delete
- **Pseudo-profundity**: "The fabric of existence weaves a tapestry..." → replace with concrete language

## Rules

- Never claim 100% human-written if AI was used
- Never publish raw AI output without human editing
- Never use AI to generate fake reviews
- Never use AI-generated author persona without disclosure
